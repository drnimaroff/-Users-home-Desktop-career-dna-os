How the MVP works

The experience is intentionally simple: capture once, review once, generate everywhere.

1. Upload your CV

Bring in a Word or PDF and let the system extract a structured, reviewable professional record.

2. Review with trust guardrails

Proposed items stay quarantined until you review and approve them. Nothing untrusted appears in documents.

3. Generate any document

Turn the same trusted baseline into an academic CV, a bio, a faculty profile, or a grant-ready document.
